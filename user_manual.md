# 📘 Store Manager User Manual

Welcome to your Shop's Digital Manager! This software is designed to replace your old notebooks (Khata), cash registers, and calculators. Here is a simple breakdown of what every feature does.

---

### 📦 1. Your Inventory Setup
Before you can sell anything, you have to tell the system what you sell.

* **Categories**: Groups for your items to keep them organized.
  * *Example: "Car Tyres", "Bike Tyres", "Engine Oil"*
* **Units**: How you count or measure the items you sell.
  * *Example: "Piece (Pcs)", "Liters (Ltr)", "Carton"*
* **Products**: The actual items you sell in your shop. You give them a buying price, selling price, and connect them to a Category and Unit.
  * *Example: "General Tyre 175/70R13" (Category: Car Tyres, Unit: Pcs)*

---

### 👥 2. People (Khata / Ledgers)
This is where you track who owes you money, and who you owe money to.

* **Customers**: The people buying from you.
  * **Walk-in Customer**: A quick button for someone paying cash who you don't need to remember.
  * **Regular Customer**: You save their name so you can give them items on credit/due (Udhaar).
* **Suppliers (Vendors)**: The companies or people *you* buy your stock from. You track their accounts here to know how much you owe them.

---

### 💵 3. Buying & Selling (The Core Features)

* **Purchases (Buying Stock)**
  * **What it does:** This is when you buy new items from your **Suppliers**.
  * **Why use it:** When you create a "Purchase", the system automatically **adds the items to your stock quantity**. If you don't pay the supplier in full, it also records the debt in the Supplier's ledger!
* **New Sale / Orders (Selling Stock)**
  * **What it does:** This is when a **Customer** buys items from you.
  * **Why use it:** When you create an "Order", the system automatically **removes the items from your stock quantity**. It creates a beautiful receipt. If the customer doesn't pay in full, the remaining amount is added to their ledger as "Due".
* **Quotations (Price Estimates)**
  * **What it does:** A customer asks "How much will 4 tyres cost?" but isn't ready to buy yet. You create a Quotation to give them a printed estimate.
  * **Why use it:** Quotations **DO NOT** remove items from your stock and **DO NOT** add debt to a customer's ledger. It is just a saved piece of paper until they come back to finalize the deal.

---

### 📉 4. Money Management

* **Expenses (Kharch)**
  * **What it does:** Track money leaving the shop that isn't for buying stock.
  * **Why use it:** Record daily tea, lunch, electricity bills, shop rent, or worker salaries. This ensures your final Profit calculations are accurate.
* **Daily Report & Dashboard**
  * **What it does:** Your financial heartbeat. It shows you how much you earned today, how much stock is running low, your total expenses, and the total amount of money trapped in market debts (Udhaar).
